# Boltjar name and logo

The code is open source (see [LICENSE-EXCEPTION.md](LICENSE-EXCEPTION.md)). The name **Boltjar**
and the Boltjar logo are not part of that license. They identify this project, so people know what
they are getting.

## Fine without asking

- Talking about Boltjar, writing tutorials, videos and reviews about it.
- Saying your pack, graph or product "works with Boltjar" or is "built with Boltjar".
- Sharing unmodified copies of Boltjar under its own name.
- Using the logo to link to this project.

## Ask first

- Using "Boltjar" or the logo in the name of a product, company, domain, or hosted service.
- Distributing a modified version under the Boltjar name. A fork is welcome, it just needs its
  own name and logo.
- Anything that suggests official endorsement.

Questions: open a discussion on the repository.
